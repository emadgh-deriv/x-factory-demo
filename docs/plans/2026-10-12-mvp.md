## Risks
- `@material/web` is web components; must render client-only. Wrapped in a small client component.
- Liveblog bodies are large; rendered as-is (approved Q2).
- Daily 500-call cap: caching via `fetch` revalidate.

## Proof
- Vitest: format, sanitize, guardian error mapping.
- `next build`, `eslint`, `tsc --noEmit`.
- Browser: desktop 1280×800, 390×844, 844×390; force states via `?__state=` not added — use real 404 id, invalid key for error, and a high page number for empty.

## Departures from the mockup (recorded during build)
1. Dark tokens are hand-written MD3 tones for the Stadium Blue seed, not generated with `material-color-utilities` (light values copied from the mockup). Full theming is backlog #3.
2. Mobile landscape is detected with `(orientation: landscape) and (max-height: 500px)`, because 844 px wide falls in MD3 "expanded" by width alone.
3. The list is restored from `sessionStorage` (one-shot, 30 min max), since App Router remounts the page on Back.
4. Article 5xx panel title is "Can't load this article" (list copy would be wrong on an article).
5. Article ids that don't look like content ids (fewer than 3 segments, or `search`/`tags`/…) show Not found without calling the API, so the route can't reach other Guardian endpoints.
6. Relative times fill in after hydration (they depend on the viewer's clock).
7. `.gitignore` added: `.env.local` was not ignored before.
